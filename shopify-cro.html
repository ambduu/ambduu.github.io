The approach

CRO that ends in shipped code

Plenty of CRO audits end as a slide deck that nobody has time to build. Because I'm a Shopify developer first, the recommendations come with an estimate and a plan for building them, and I can do the build on your theme, your checkout extensions or your apps.

I don't promise a percentage uplift before seeing your data. Every store, product and audience is different. What I do promise is a clear list of problems, ranked by likely impact and effort, and honest measurement of what changed.

Step one

The Shopify CRO audit

  • Funnel review in Shopify analytics: sessions, add-to-cart, reached checkout and completed orders, split by device, channel and landing page.
  • Template review of the home page, collections, product pages, search, cart and checkout, on real phones as well as desktop.
  • Technical checks: page speed, layout shift, broken variant logic, app conflicts, console errors and tracking that double-counts or misses events.
  • A ranked action list of quick wins, larger changes and ideas worth testing, each with an effort estimate.
Testing

A/B testing, done honestly

A/B tests are the best way to know whether a change works, but only when the store has enough traffic and orders to reach a reliable result. For high-traffic stores I set up tests with a clear hypothesis, one meaningful change and a success metric agreed up front.

For smaller stores, running a test for weeks to prove an obvious fix is wasted time. There I ship well-established improvements, such as clearer pricing, fewer steps or faster pages, and compare before and after over a sensible period, with the caveats spelled out.

Where the gains usually are

Product page, cart and checkout improvements

Product pages

Variant pickers that make sense, sold-out and back-in-stock states, delivery and returns information near the add-to-cart button, images that load fast, and a buy button that stays reachable on mobile.

Cart

A cart drawer that updates without a page reload, editable quantities, a free-shipping progress bar where it fits your pricing, and relevant cross-sells that don't slow the page down.

Checkout

On Shopify Plus, Checkout UI extensions can add trust messages, delivery notes or upsells in the right step, and Shopify Functions can apply discount, delivery and payment rules without fragile workarounds. Express payment buttons and a clean, distraction-free checkout matter on every plan. More on this on the Shopify Plus page.

Speed is a conversion lever

Faster pages, fewer drop-offs

A product page that takes too long to show its main image or respond to a tap loses shoppers before they ever see your offer. Speed work is often the highest-leverage part of a CRO project, which is why it has its own page: Shopify speed optimization. For the release side, see speed and launch.

Common questions

Shopify CRO, answered

What is Shopify conversion rate optimization?

It is the work of increasing the share of visitors who place an order, by finding where shoppers drop off in the funnel and fixing the causes: unclear product pages, a confusing cart, a slow site or friction at checkout.

What does a CRO audit include?

A funnel review in Shopify analytics, a template-by-template review of the store on mobile and desktop, technical checks for speed, errors and tracking, and a ranked list of fixes with effort estimates.

Do you run A/B tests?

Yes, when the store has enough traffic and orders for a test to reach a reliable result. For smaller stores I ship proven improvements and compare before and after instead.

Can you improve the checkout on Shopify?

On Shopify Plus, Checkout UI extensions and Shopify Functions allow trust content, upsells and custom discount, delivery and payment rules. On other plans, the focus is the cart, express payments and the steps before checkout.

Do you also implement the changes?

Yes. I'm a Shopify developer, so the fixes from the audit can be built on your theme, checkout extensions or apps, then measured.
