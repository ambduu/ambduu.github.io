The process

How a project runs

  1. Brief. You share what you need, any designs, and access to the store or a development store.
  2. Review and estimate. I look at the current theme, apps and data, ask the awkward questions early, and send a written scope.
  3. Build. Work happens on a separate theme or development store, in Git, with Shopify CLI. You get preview links as features land, not just at the end.
  4. QA and launch. Testing across devices and browsers, a launch checklist, and a release you can roll back if needed.
  5. After launch. Fixes, handover notes and, if you want it, ongoing support.
What you get

What you get at the end

  • Clean, commented Liquid and JavaScript, built on Online Store 2.0 sections and blocks your team can edit in the theme editor.
  • The code in your own repository, with no lock-in: any developer can pick it up after me.
  • Fewer apps where custom code is lighter and cheaper, and theme app extensions where an app is the right answer.

Common questions

Hiring a Shopify developer, answered

How much does it cost to hire a Shopify developer?

It depends on the scope. Small fixes and improvements are usually billed by time, while new themes, Shopify Plus checkout work and integrations get a fixed quote after I've reviewed the store and the brief.

Can you take over an existing theme or another developer's code?

Yes. I start with a review of the theme, apps and custom code, point out what's risky, and then work on it in Git so every change is traceable.

Do you work with Shopify Plus?

Yes. Checkout UI extensions, Shopify Functions for discounts, delivery and payments, B2B, Shopify Markets and migrations away from checkout.liquid and Shopify Scripts.

Do you work with agencies on a white-label basis?

Yes. A good part of my work has been as a contract developer inside agency teams, using their repositories, ticketing tools and review process.

What time zone are you in?

I'm in Portugal, on the same time as London. That gives full overlap with the UK, near-full overlap with the rest of Europe, and shared hours with the US East Coast in the morning.

How quickly can you start?

Send me the brief and your timeline. I'll tell you honestly when I can start and whether the deadline is realistic.
