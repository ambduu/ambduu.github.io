Europe and beyond

Working with brands and agencies across Europe

Selling across Europe brings its own checklist: several languages and currencies on one store, prices shown the way local shoppers expect, cookie consent that doesn't break tracking, and shipping rules that change at every border. Shopify Markets, Functions and a well-structured theme handle most of it, as long as it's planned from the start rather than bolted on later.

The stores I've worked on include German brands like Maitea, Recolution and Bübchen, Shopify Plus stores such as Zugu Case, Aavrani and Wool & Oak, and custom builds like Abstrax Tech and Cheribundi. I've also worked as a contract developer inside agency teams, including CTI Digital (UK), Helping Brands (Germany), Elev8 Experience (US) and Uplers (India). You can browse the full list of stores.
